Accessibility at
Home Filla

We're working toward a world where everyone has a safe place to call home. We believe that removing barriers to access benefits all when it comes to the business of buying, selling, owning, or renting a home.

Digital Accessibility

Home Filla is committed to making our website accessible for all users, including those with disabilities.

WCAG Compliance

We strive to meet Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA standards across our digital properties.

Continuous Improvement

We regularly audit our site and work with accessibility experts to identify and address barriers.

User Feedback

Your feedback helps us improve. If you encounter any accessibility barriers, please contact us.

Our Commitment to Inclusion

At Home Filla, we believe everyone deserves equal access to housing information and services. Our commitment to accessibility extends beyond compliance — it's about creating an inclusive experience for all users.

What We're Doing

  • Regular accessibility audits and testing
  • Training our teams on accessibility best practices
  • Implementing accessible design patterns
  • Providing alternative formats for content when needed
  • Working with assistive technologies

Third-Party Content

While we strive for accessibility across our platform, some third-party content may not fully meet our standards. We encourage our partners to prioritize accessibility in their offerings.

Accessibility Features

Our website includes the following accessibility features:

Keyboard Navigation

All interactive elements are accessible via keyboard navigation.

Screen Reader Support

We use semantic HTML and ARIA labels to ensure compatibility with screen readers.

Color Contrast

Text and background colors meet WCAG contrast ratio requirements.

Resizable Text

Text can be resized up to 200% without loss of content or functionality.

Alt Text for Images

Meaningful images include descriptive alternative text.

Consistent Navigation

Navigation is consistent across pages to help users orient themselves.
