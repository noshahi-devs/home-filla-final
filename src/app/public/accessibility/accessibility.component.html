Accessibility at Home Filla

At Home Filla, we're working toward a world where everyone has a safe place to call home. We believe that removing barriers to access benefits all when it comes to the business of buying, selling, owning, or renting a home.

It's up to all of us, working together to create the future we want to live in.

We're serious about accessibility

Digital accessibility standards

Home Filla uses W3C Web Content Accessibility Guidelines 2.1 AA or higher for guidance. We also invest in automated testing tools and conduct on-going, third-party audits to continually improve our experiences.

Inclusive design

Our dedicated Accessibility team provides processes, tools, training, education, and research that product, design, and engineering need to build products that everyone can use.

We value your feedback

If you encounter any accessibility barriers on our digital properties, please let us know so we can address them.
