Frequently asked questions

What is the average time a home stays on the market?
The national average time a home stays on the market, often referred to as "days on market" (DOM), can vary depending on factors like location, market conditions, and time of year. As of recent, the national average tends to be around 30 to 60 days, but this can be significantly shorter or longer depending on your local real estate market. To find out the specific DOM for your home or in your area, you can check resources like My Home dashboard, or consult a real estate agent who can provide market data specific to your neighborhood.
How can I estimate my home’s value?

To estimate your home's value, you can work with a real estate agent who can provide a comparative market analysis (CMA). A CMA involves analyzing recently sold homes in your area with similar characteristics (size, condition, location) to give you an estimate. Real estate agents also consider current market trends, property features, and neighborhood demand when estimating value.

Alternatively, My Home dashboard can offer instant home value estimates. We call these "RealEstimate" and they are based on public records data. While these estimates can provide a rough idea of your home's worth, they may not account for unique factors like condition or upgrades, which is why consulting an agent for a more personalized assessment can be beneficial.

What are some ways to increase my home’s value?

There are several effective ways to increase your home's value, starting with improvements that enhance both its functionality and curb appeal:

  1. Updating the kitchen and bathrooms often provides the best return on investment.
  2. Upgrading appliances, installing modern fixtures, and adding fresh paint.
  3. Curb appeal is crucial; simple landscaping improvements or a new front door can boost first impressions.
  4. Energy-efficient upgrades, such as new windows or smart home features.
  5. Finishing a basement or attic can add valuable living space.

By focusing on these high-impact areas, you can increase your home's appeal and market value.

How can I find home values near me?
  • One of the easiest ways is to use the My Home dashboard which provides estimated home values and detailed information on recently sold similar homes in your area.
  • For a more precise understanding, you can contact a local real estate agent who can give you a comparative market analysis (CMA).
  • Additionally, county assessor websites sometimes provide property tax records that include the last sale price of homes in your area.
