Understanding Ad Choices

Home Filla, Inc. and its affiliates ("Company," "we," "us") are committed to providing you with transparency and control over the advertising you see. This Ad Choices page explains how we deliver personalized advertising and the choices you have regarding your advertising experience.

Please review our full Privacy Notice to understand our complete privacy practices.

What Are Personalized Ads?

Personalized ads (also known as targeted ads or interest-based ads) are advertisements that are tailored to your interests based on information collected about your online activities. These ads may appear on our Services or on third-party websites and apps.

Personalized ads help us show you content that may be more relevant to you, including real estate listings, mortgage offers, home improvement services, and other products and services related to your interests.

How We Use Your Information for Advertising

We and our advertising partners collect information to deliver personalized ads, including:

  • Your browsing activity on our Services
  • Your search queries and saved listings
  • Information about properties you view or save
  • Your device information and IP address
  • Location information
  • Information from third-party partners

We use this information to infer your interests and deliver ads that may be relevant to you. For example, if you frequently search for homes in a specific area, we may show you ads for real estate agents or mortgage lenders in that area.

Your Ad Choices

You have several options to control personalized advertising:

1. Opt Out of Personalized Advertising

You can opt out of receiving personalized ads from Home Filla by:

  • Visiting your account settings and updating your privacy preferences
  • Clicking the "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" link in our footer
  • Using industry opt-out tools described below

2. Industry Opt-Out Tools

You can opt out of personalized advertising from participating companies through these industry programs:

  • Digital Advertising Alliance (DAA) - Opt out of interest-based advertising from participating companies
  • Network Advertising Initiative (NAI) - Opt out of targeted advertising from NAI members
  • Mobile App Choices - Control interest-based ads in mobile apps

Please note that opting out does not mean you will stop seeing ads. You will still see ads, but they may be less relevant to your interests.

3. Browser and Device Controls

Most web browsers and mobile devices offer privacy settings that can help you control targeted advertising:

  • Do Not Track (DNT): You can enable DNT in your browser settings
  • Cookie Settings: You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings
  • Mobile Device Settings: iOS and Android devices allow you to reset your advertising identifier and opt out of interest-based ads

Third-Party Advertising Partners

We work with third-party advertising companies to deliver personalized ads. These partners may collect information about your online activities over time and across different websites and apps to deliver targeted advertising.

Our advertising partners may use cookies, web beacons, and other tracking technologies to collect information. They may combine this information with data they have collected from other sources.

Types of Ads We Deliver

We and our partners may deliver various types of ads, including:

  • Interest-Based Ads: Based on your inferred interests and online activities
  • Retargeting Ads: Shown to you after you have visited our Services
  • Contextual Ads: Based on the content you are currently viewing
  • Geographic Ads: Based on your general location
  • First-Party Ads: Based on your activity within our Services

California Privacy Rights

California residents have specific rights regarding the sale or sharing of personal information for targeted advertising purposes. For more information, please review our Privacy Notice or visit our California Privacy Rights page.

To opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information, click the "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" link in the footer of our website.
