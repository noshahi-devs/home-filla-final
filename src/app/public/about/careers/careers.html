    We’re just built differently—and that’s a good thing

    If your mission is to completely redefine the way people find home in every form, you need to act, think, and see things differently. You need to be different.

    Welcome to Home Filla.

    Values

    What we’re all about

    At Home Filla, we’re using tech to make challenging things easier for everyone. But for our employees, it all boils down to a few fairly simple ideas.

    Build it best

    We’re driven to be the best and to challenge the status quo at the forefront of the digital real estate space. Are you ready to set ambitious goals and achieve them? We’ll give you what you need to be innovative and revolutionize how people buy, sell, rent, and own their homes.

    Bring people home

    Our reason for being is to give everyone the opportunity to confidently find the right place for them. We build trust with consumers and enable fairness, respect, transparency, and equity. Everyone deserves a place they can call home, raise a family, create memories, and celebrate all of life’s major milestones.

    Be your best

    Our culture is deeply rooted in both enabling and celebrating your unique skills, knowledge, and excellence. You’ll feel challenged and inspired by teammates and leaders alike to constantly grow, develop, and be at your best as we work together as a team.

    Pride of ownership

    At Home Filla, where you take your career—and what you achieve—is up to you. Here, you’ll think big and make meaningful things happen. Together, we’ll set big goals—then we’ll give you the freedom to do what you do best to reach them.

    Beliefs

    What we do is defined by the way we do it

    At Home Filla, we’re united by a set of beliefs that inform and inspire every action we take, and every interaction we make. Our ambition sets us apart. We compete ferociously and know that becoming No.1. will be the result of securing many smaller victories. Budgets establish our baseline, but we set stretch goals that push us beyond market growth and make transformational bets that create step-changes in performance.

    We are a technology company in the midst of constant technological change. We value innovation and are motivated by a desire to lead that change rather than a fear of being left behind. Our innovation is honed on the needs our customers have today and the ones we anticipate they’ll have tomorrow. We certain that innovation is most often ignited through collaboration.

    We believe in the power of partnership. We curate and cultivate relationships that bring diversity of thought and unique strengths to create competitive advantage. This collaborative spirit extends outside our four walls to our community of customers, partners, and stakeholders. We value those who energize the people in their orbit, and those who can engineer better outcomes because of the way they have engaged with them.

    Our leaders empower their team members to be accountable and successful in their role, in the same way our team members embrace their agency and ownership of that role. We are individually accountable but collectively dependent on one another, embracing dual obligations to keep track of dependencies we rely upon. We value outcomes over effort and hold ourselves and others accountable to that measure.

    Our DEI&P Promise

    Welcoming everyone home

    At Home Filla, we embrace our passion for building a way home for everyone with a Diversity, Equity, Inclusion, and Philanthropy strategy that:

    • Supports and celebrates a diverse workforce of talented people
    • Champions inclusive leaders
    • Partners with and services diverse and inclusive communities
    • Enables and empowers our consumers and customers

    We believe that when people feel respected and included, they can be more creative, innovative, and successful. Around here, there are no ceilings – glass or otherwise – to keep you from being your genuine self and achieving your highest aspirations.

    Our hiring process

    We want to get to know the real you. Here’s a look at the steps we’ll take together to see if Home Filla is the right place for your next career move.

    1

    Application

    Find a role that matches your skills and passions. Submit your application through our careers site to get started on your journey.

    2

    Recruiter Screening

    If your profile is a match, one of our recruiters will reach out for a brief conversation to learn more about your experience and career goals.

    3

    Interviews

    Meet with the hiring team to dive deeper into your technical skills and how you align with our values. This is also your chance to get to know us!

    4

    Offer

    Congratulations! If we find a mutual match, we’ll extend an offer for you to join our team and start building a better way home.
