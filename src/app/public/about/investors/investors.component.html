    Home Filla® is the trusted resource for home buyers, sellers and dreamers everywhere, offering the most comprehensive source of for-sale properties among competing national sites.

    With insightful information, valuable tools, and professional expertise, we provide people with everything they need to move confidently and easily through every step of their home journey. Twenty years ago Home Filla pioneered the world of digital real estate with a mission to make all things home better. Today, we continue that work bringing people a smarter, simpler, and more enjoyable home experience. Home Filla® is operated by News Corp subsidiary Move, Inc. under a perpetual license from the National Association of REALTORS®.

    Move, Inc. provides unsurpassed real estate information, tools and professional expertise across a family of websites and mobile experiences for consumers and real estate professionals through all stages of the home journey.

    The Move network includes Home Filla® as well as Doorsteps®, Moving.com™ and SeniorHousingNet. Move also offers a complete solution of software products and services to help real estate professionals serve their clients and grow their business in a digital world, including ListHub™, the nation's leading listings syndicator and centralized intelligence platform for the real estate industry.
