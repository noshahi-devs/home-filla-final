About Ad Choices

Last Updated: April 15, 2026

Interest-Based Advertising

Interest-based advertising, also known as online behavioral advertising, uses information collected across multiple websites and applications to predict your preferences and show you ads that are most likely to be of interest to you.

Home Filla® works with various advertising partners to deliver personalized advertisements on our site and across the internet. This process often involves the use of cookies and other tracking technologies.

How to Opt-Out

If you prefer not to receive interest-based advertising, you can opt-out in several ways:

  • Digital Advertising Alliance (DAA): Visit the DAA Consumer Choice Page to manage your preferences for participating companies.
  • Network Advertising Initiative (NAI): Use the NAI Opt-Out Tool.
  • Browser Settings: Most web browsers allow you to block or delete cookies through their settings menu.
  • Mobile Devices: On iOS and Android, you can adjust your "Limit Ad Tracking" or "Opt out of Interest-Based Ads" settings.

Note: Opting out of interest-based advertising does not mean you will no longer see ads. It simply means those ads will not be personalized based on your browsing history.

Our Commitment

Home Filla is committed to transparency and providing you with meaningful choices regarding your privacy. We comply with the Self-Regulatory Principles for Online Behavioral Advertising as managed by the DAA.

For more information on how we handle your data, please see our Privacy Policy.
