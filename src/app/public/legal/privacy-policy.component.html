Overview

At Home Filla®, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and share information in connection with your use of our websites, mobile applications, and other services.

By using the Home Filla services, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with these terms, please do not use our services.

Key Change: We have updated our data sharing sections to clarify how we work with real estate professionals to provide you with better search results.

Information We Collect

We collect information about you in several ways, including:

  • Directly from you: Such as when you create an account, save a search, or contact an agent.
  • Automatically: Through cookies, web beacons, and other tracking technologies as you navigate the site.
  • From third parties: Such as social media platforms or real estate professional partners.

This information may include your name, email address, phone number, saved properties, and search preferences.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to provide, maintain, and improve our services, including:

  1. Personalizing your home search experience.
  2. Facilitating communication between you and real estate professionals.
  3. Sending you property alerts and marketing communications (with your consent).
  4. Analyzing site usage to improve functionality and performance.

Information Sharing

We may share your information with:

  • Real Estate Professionals: When you request information about a property or agent.
  • Service Providers: Who perform services on our behalf.
  • Affiliates: Within the News Corp family of companies.
  • Legal Authorities: When required by law or to protect our rights.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties for their own marketing purposes without your explicit consent.

Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access and disclosure. However, no data transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

Your Choices & Rights

You have choices regarding how your information is used. You can:

  • Access and update your account information at any time.
  • Opt-out of marketing emails by clicking "unsubscribe."
  • Adjust your browser settings to decline cookies.
  • Contact us to request deletion of your account.

For more information, please visit our Ad Choices page.
